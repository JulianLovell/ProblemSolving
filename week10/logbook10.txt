Problem 1 - DifferentStrings
Design Ideas:
I think i need ot do this in 2 different steps. There will be a step to get the 2 words to be the same length, choosing and placing letters in a way that maximises the number in the right position.
The next step will be to then iterate over both strings, with a counter that will increase +1 with every index that does not match.
The bit i am struggling with is how to select the letter to add, and then the logic to decide if it goes on the front or end of the string.

Troubleshooting:
I overcomplicated my approach by trying to manually extend A to match the length of B, character by character. This made is difficult to find and optimal solution. After rethinking, i realised that 
a better approach was to compare A against all possible subtrings of B that are of the same length as A. This simplified the process and allowed for a simpler calculation of the min difference.

Reflection:
Switching to a sliding window approach made the problem much easier to solve. By focussing on comparing A directly with subtrings of B, i avoided unnecessary complexity and was able to detirmine the minimum
number of differences. The key here was understanding that extending A step by step was really a waste of time, instead, finding the optimal alignment of A with a subtring of B was better.\
